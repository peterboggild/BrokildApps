THE LEGEND

WHAT THE MARKS MEAN. YOU FOUND IT, SO YOU MAY KEEP IT.

THE FIVE CELLS

SKRA — a geiger cloud. ALEPH is how often it ticks (0.3 → 2600 a second), BETH the size and pitch of each tick.

VELM — two sines modulating each other's frequency. ALEPH is pitch and ratio at once, BETH the depth. Past about 60 % it stops being periodic at all.

ONDA — pink noise through three band-passes that wander. ALEPH is where they sit, BETH how tight they are and how fast they roam.

KRYS — five inharmonic strings. ALEPH tunes them, BETH is decay, up to nearly forever. They are struck by SKRA whether or not SKRA is switched on.

GORR — eats the room's own output: sample-rate crusher, bit crusher, wavefolder. ALEPH destroys, BETH folds and decides how much of the room it is fed. This is the feedback loop; it is why the machine can surprise you.

PORT — the door

A resonant filter with four voices, played over MIDI or from the keys.
WHEEL cutoff · TEETH resonance, it sings above ~90 % · SHAPE low / band / high / notch · TRACK how much a played note owns the pitch — at 100 % it is in tune, at 0 the notes only open the envelope · CREEP portamento · ONSET / LETGO the envelope · RESIDUE the untriggered drone that is always open · SWEEP envelope to cutoff, downward below 50 % and upward above · TWIN a second resonance 0–24 semitones up, TWIN·M its level.

THE TRAPS

SKIRV — slice repeater. CHANCE is odds and mix in one, SHARD is the slice. It reverses and double-speeds at random.
DRAUP — delay. DEPTH is time, RETURN is feedback and level in one knob, GRAIN tilts the tone inside the loop.
MJØL — a four-line reverb. SPACE is size and tail, VEIL is mix, ASCENT feeds an octave-up shimmer back in.
GABB saturates, UTVEI is the output. A limiter sits after both — it has to.

SEGL — the lock

Six bits, sixty-four rooms. The sigil seeds eight wires; each listens to something and moves something else. The same sigil always builds the same eight wires, so a room you liked can be found again. The KART below remembers which ones you have been in.

BIND is how hard the wires pull. At zero the room is inert and every knob does exactly what it says — that is the way to learn it.
DRIFT adds a slow drunk walk to everything, wired or not.
PULSE is the speed of the chaos core and of the drift.

READING THE LOOM

A solid arc with a filled spark pushes its destination up; a dashed arc with a hollow spark pulls it down. The colour is the source. A ring node swells while it is being moved and glows while it is making sound. The dotted ghost arc outside a knob is where the loom has actually put that knob this instant.
