- **Six measured lessons (the DSP ones generalise to every FDN in the fleet):**
  1. **A block read must advance within the block.** The whole sub-block was written into the source line and then every sample read at the same delay from the block's END — nothing ever arrived. Found by reading the slot's history buffer, not the code (taps fine, gain fine, history all zero).
  2. **Two coupled FDNs with long decays cannot be coupled both ways**: modal peaks sit tens of dB above the mean gain and a coincident pair exceeds unity whatever the energy-balance coefficient (a PLASTER hall ran away at +40 dB/s with k=0.19). Coupling is a DAG down the source-power ranking; the double slope survives through the door-field paths.
  3. **Any interpolation inside a recirculating delay is a lowpass applied hundreds of times a second.** Linear interpolation of a 0.7-sample modulation cost 3–5× of the late field's energy and shortened every 4 kHz decay by a quarter. Integer delays in the loop; Hermite for one-shot reads.
  4. **G = fs·RT/(13.8·L) is right only for a well-mixed network**: a plain Hadamard is an involution and an injection pattern summing to zero part-hides from the sum observer (measured 0.6 of the formula). Sign-flipped mixing + an observer with its own signs + the efficiency MEASURED at prepare (impulse in, pair energy out).
  5. **A first-order shelf realises ~80 % of its gain one octave above its corner**: the 4-anchor loss filter is fitted iteratively on the true digital response (`shelfDb`), else ABSORBING decays came out 40 % long.
  6. **JUCE hands a choice parameter's RAW value as its index, not a 0..1 fraction** — the first live build read every room as TILED, caught by the RT numbers in the scene stream, not by any bench.
- **Four bench probes lied before the code did** (the vacuous-window family, again): a 4 ms window cannot resolve 250 Hz; the listener-room weight ramping from zero over 0.2 s stretched short decays; an impulse 10 samples in landed inside a fresh path's 2.7 ms gain ramp and read as a near-field loss; a test source walking straight THROUGH the listener's head "clicked". And a max-sample-step metric on a moving pure tone measures the tone's own slope changing — a click is broadband, so the metric is energy above 6 kHz.
