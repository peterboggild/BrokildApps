BRAIN SCAN

Store a volume, not a waveform, and read it along a line. A specimen is a scalar field in a cube. A scan line is a curve through it, and one cycle of the waveform is the field along that curve. The filter's cutoff and the modulator are the same thing read slower — two more lines. SCAN blends the geometry of two lines and only then reads the tissue, so halfway between two sounds is a place neither of them has been.

Lines are drawn on the tomography plane: drag a point to move it, click the tissue to add one, alt-click to remove. The gantry is for looking.
