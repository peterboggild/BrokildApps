Intact. Recovered as one of a matched pair; the second body remains in situ.

Found in a dry cistern at the head of a rille, two bodies set facing one another across a gap of four metres and held at that separation by nothing anyone could see. Okonkwo-Reyes was surveying the cistern for relict brine and logged them as concretions. She revised the entry the same evening: on lifting the first, the second changed pitch.

The pair is the reason the survey went looking for more. One hundred and twenty-three days later, at the Sabik Terminator on the far side of the twilight band, it found sixty-seven of them laid out on an eightfold plan — catalogued as B2311.67 and following.
