Hairfryer

Sing clean. Leave fried.

A vocal strip that converts a clean, loud singing voice into a metal one — growls, gutturals, pig squeals, fry — by wrecking the part of the voice a distortion pedal can never reach: the glottal source, underneath your own vowels. No AI, no voice model, nobody else's scream. Your voice is the only voice in the room.

Why a pedal can't growl, and this can

A sung note is a source — the glottal pulses — played through a filter: your throat and mouth, whose resonances are the vowels. A pedal distorts the filter. A screamer distorts the source.

Put distortion on a whole voice and the formants intermodulate; the vowel goes to mush and the ear says “voice through a pedal”. A real growler leaves the vocal tract alone — that is why you can hear the words — and drives the false folds above the true folds into period-doubled or chaotic vibration. The pitch survives, because the pitch is the true folds. The monster is subharmonics and pulse-locked turbulence, wearing the singer's own vowels.

The Hairfryer does the same operation to a signal. An order-18 LPC lattice splits the incoming voice into source and filter in real time. The source is wrecked — see below — and then synthesised back through your own vocal tract. Everything it adds comes out shaped by your formants. Your vowels. Your words. Your melody.

Measured, not hoped. The release bench sings a synthetic vowel through the plugin and measures the claim: the input's vowel contrast is 35.2 dB, the growled output keeps 35.0 dB of it, and the same voice through a fuzz pedal collapses to 19.7 dB. The sung pitch beats its off-pitch neighbours by 40 dB after the full guttural treatment. That pair of numbers is the entire reason this plugin exists.
Real chaos

The CHAOS knob is a bifurcation diagram

One iteration of the logistic map per detected glottal period. The knob walks the true cascade — steady, period-2 rasp, the period-three guttural window (it has a plateau so you can find it), chaos. The panel shows which regime it measures.

False folds

A second voice that locks to yours

A resonator with a saturator in its feedback, driven by your voice. Turned up it self-oscillates and entrains to f/2, f/3 or f/4 of whatever you sing — biphonation, the two-voices-at-once quality of a real growl.

Pulse-locked rasp

Noise that breaks when you do

Turbulence fired at your glottal closures, shaped by your formants, gated by the same chaos as the gain — so it fuses into the voice instead of sitting on top as hiss. Silence in, silence out: measured 0.000001 RMS.

Warped LPC

THROAT resizes you

Formants slide ±35% while the pitch stays put. Down is a bigger chest for dooms and gutturals; up is the pig squeal. Two racks cook at once — growl under, shriek over.

Nine programmes, like a microwave

Every programme is a complete patch, and the LCD narrates everything you touch. CLEAN PASS and WARM STRIP leave the fryer off — the strip alone is a serviceable vocal channel.

And a proper strip around it

The voice needs to survive the treatment, so the appliance carries the whole channel.

PREP

Gate with hold, fourth-order low cut, and a de-esser that judges sibilance by the ratio of high band to everything — a singer getting louder is not an ess. Measured: a bare ess ducked 18.8 dB, a vowel through the same settings moved 0.00 dB.

HEAT

An asymmetric tube stage (even harmonics — warmth) with a SPARKLE exciter on top, oversampled up to 4× at constant latency, and RMS-matched around itself so DRIVE is density, not volume. At zero it is measured transparent.

SEASONING

Four bands of RBJ equaliser: low shelf, two parametric mids, high shelf.

Level honesty

The wet path is continuously gain-matched to the dry voice, so BLEND compares character, not loudness — the growl cannot win the argument by being louder (measured within 0.2 dB).

Download and install

Two minutes. There is no installer — a VST3 plugin is a folder you copy into place.

  1. Download the zip. Hairfryer-VST3-win64.zip (7 MB).
  2. Unzip it. Right-click → Extract All. Inside you get a Hairfryer.vst3 folder, a standalone Hairfryer.exe, the manual and a readme.
  3. Copy the plugin folder into the VST3 directory. Move the whole Hairfryer.vst3 folder — not just the file inside it — into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\. Windows will ask for administrator permission; that is normal.
  4. Rescan plugins in your DAW. In Ableton Live: Preferences → Plug-Ins → Rescan. It appears as an effect called Hairfryer, by Brokild. Put it on a vocal channel, pick FALSE CORD, and commit.
If the plugin window is blank, install the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime. The interface is drawn with WebView2, which ships with current Windows but can be missing on older or heavily stripped installations. The audio keeps working with the window shut either way.
