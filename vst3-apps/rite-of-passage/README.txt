RITE OF PASSAGE  -  a Brokild transition processor  -  build 260922.1

A transition is not an effect. It is a journey with a landing, and the landing
is the part everyone remembers.

One slider is the journey. Six slots hold any of eighteen transition effects -
duplicates allowed, and the slots are the chain order - and each slot carries
two complete settings plus where on the travel it starts moving, on what curve,
and how far it actually gets. So a build is written as a score you read left to
right, and the panel catches fire along it as the slider crosses.

The landing is its own command. ARRIVAL is a trigger you fire, not the end of
the slider, so you can scrub while editing without setting off a drop. It lands
sample-accurately on the bar, restores the dry signal in one block, fires a
tuned sub, releases the mono gate - and every slot decides for itself whether
it STOPS dead, SPILLS its tail over the downbeat, or CLEARS to a vacuum.

The Brokild World FX rack is in here twice: as a stage after the six slots with
its five macros sequenceable along the travel, and as ten modules you can drop
INTO a slot, from their own submenu on every lane. In a slot they get an order,
a place in the stereo field, and a tail mode - a GATE working the sides only, an
ECHO ringing on through a drop that clears everything around it.

At rest it is not there at all: an empty rite is bit-transparent, and latency is
zero.

INSTALLING

  Copy the folder  Rite of Passage.vst3  into
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  and rescan in your host.

READ THIS FIRST

  A slot you have just assigned does nothing, and that is deliberate: both
  sides of it start at the effect's own defaults, so A equals B and nothing
  travels. Click the lane, and in the B row move the thing you want to arrive
  at. The lane stays hatched until you do.

WHAT IT AUTOMATES

  Twelve parameters and no more: POSITION (the slider), ARRIVAL (the trigger),
  MIX, OUTPUT, SPREAD, TURN, MONO GATE, and the World rack's five macros.
  Everything else - six slot assignments, two full settings each, enter, exit,
  curve, depth, place, tail mode - rides in the plugin's own state, so
  re-assigning a slot can never orphan an automation lane.
