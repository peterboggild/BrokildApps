Why parts and not a panel

An image generator cannot hit exact pixel coordinates. Ask for a whole faceplate with ninety-six knob holes registered to a layout and it comes back looking right and being thirty pixels out everywhere — no amount of prompt detail fixes that.

What it is superb at is a single isolated object on a transparent background. So: it makes the parts, and I assemble them at the coordinates the panel already uses. That is also why every size below is exact — a knob cap delivered at the wrong aspect gets squashed, and you will see it.

  1. Paste part 00 into a new ChatGPT conversation. It sets the world once.
  2. Then paste parts 01–17, one message each. Ask for PNG with transparency.
  3. Save each with the filename on its card, into a folder called art next to the plugin source.
  4. Tell me they are there. I composite, re-measure and send back a screenshot.

If it comes back wrong: the two failures worth re-rolling for are a drop shadow baked in (it will double up against the panel's own lighting) and perspective — anything shot at an angle cannot be used, because the panel is dead flat-on.
