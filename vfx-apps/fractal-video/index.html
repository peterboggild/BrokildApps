Scene preview shows the selected scene on its own, so you can shape it without the song moving under you. Timeline preview shows what will be rendered at the playhead: sections, transitions and the modulation matrix. Pressing play in Scene mode auditions how the selected scene reacts to the music.

Double-click any slider label to reset it. Drag a scene card onto a timeline section to assign it. Audio files are kept in this browser (IndexedDB) so they come back after a reload.
