# FULL METAL RACKET — panel decal brief

Paste this whole file to ChatGPT as one message. It is self-contained: the
style rules, all seventeen parts with exact pixel sizes and filenames, and
where to commit them.

---

## The task

Generate seventeen photographic component decals for the front panel of a
1980s-style analogue drum machine called **FULL METAL RACKET**, then commit
them to GitHub. One isolated object per image, PNG with transparency.

## The machine

Cream painted steel faceplate `#e7dcc3`, walnut wood end cheeks `#6d4526`,
black anodised sub-panels `#191307`, silver aluminium knobs with matte red
pointers `#d8412a`, amber indicator lamps `#ff8f1f`. Think Roland TR-808 and
Korg Electribe, photographed straight on under soft even studio light.
Slightly used, not battered. No branding and no lettering anywhere unless a
part explicitly asks for it.

## Rules that apply to EVERY part, without exception

- Perfectly **flat-on and orthographic**. No perspective, no tilt, no angle.
- **Transparent background** (PNG with alpha). Nothing behind the object.
- **No drop shadow and no cast shadow of any kind.** Self-shading on the
  object itself is wanted; shadow falling away from it is not.
- The object **fills the frame edge to edge** at the exact pixel dimensions
  given, with no padding or margin unless the part says otherwise.
- Even, soft, neutral studio lighting from slightly above.
- Photoreal. Not illustration, not stylised, not obviously 3D-rendered.
- Deliver at **exactly** the pixel size given. Aspect ratio matters most —
  a part delivered at the wrong aspect gets squashed on the panel and it
  shows immediately.
