JOURNEY TO THE MOON

The Moon's first test views (not a chapter)

The chapters. Surface stands you in the Taurus-Littrow valley beside the LM (chapter 6 is the way there from the landing); the two orbit views were the first tests of the Moon. NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data, lit as at the Apollo 17 landing: the Sun 13° above the eastern horizon.

On this screen: drag to look, WASD to walk on the surface, 123 views, [] Sun earlier or later, P pause the orbit.
In the headset: A or X next view, left stick walks, right stick turns, hold the right trigger to aim a teleport (right stick forward throws it further, out to the mountains) and let go to jump, B or Y hides the frame-rate panel. Your boots leave prints; you hear your steps through the suit.

The LM is the one you land in; a more detailed one comes later. The Earth overhead is NASA's Blue Marble with real clouds. Data: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University (LRO), public domain.
