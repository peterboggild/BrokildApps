Brown is the deep one — distant surf, the hum of a cabin at altitude — and masks the most for the least hiss. Green sits in the middle of the band, near 500 Hz, where wind in trees lives. Rain is a wash of far-off drops with nearer ones pattering through it; soft rain is the same weather heard through a closed window. Ocean rises and falls on its own swell. Run one on its own, or lay several over each other — brown under soft rain is where most people end up.
