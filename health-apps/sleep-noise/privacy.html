Sleeper AgentPrivacy policy

BrokildApps

Last updated 9 September 2026. Applies to Sleeper Agent for iOS and iPadOS, and to the web version at this address.

Sleeper Agent collects nothing about you. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network connections of any kind — so there is nothing for it to send anywhere, and nowhere for it to send it.

What is collected

Nothing. No personal information, no identifiers, no location, no contacts, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no advertising identifier, no analytics events. Not anonymised, not aggregated, not "pseudonymised" — none of it, because none of it is gathered in the first place.

Why that is not just a promise

The app contains no code that can reach a network. There is no HTTP request, no WebSocket, no analytics library, no advertising framework and no crash-reporting service anywhere in it. It asks the operating system for no permissions at all: not the microphone, not the camera, not your location, not notifications, not your contacts, not health data.

You can check this without taking anyone's word for it: turn on airplane mode, switch off Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and use the app normally. Everything works, because everything it does happens on your device.

What is stored on your device

Two things, both of which stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app:

WhatWhy
Your settings
the five fader levels, volume, tone, swell, sleep-timer preference, fade lengths, ambient theme and level, and the blackout preference		 So the app opens as you left it. Stored under the key brokild-sleep-noise.
Generated audio loops
the app's own synthesised sound, cached as files		 So the sound starts immediately instead of being generated again every evening. In the system cache, which the operating system may clear at any time; the app simply regenerates it.

Neither contains anything about you. They are your own preferences and the app's own audio. Neither is uploaded, synced, or shared, and neither is readable by anyone but you.

Where the sound comes from

All of it is generated on your device, sample by sample, as it plays. There are no audio recordings in the app and nothing is streamed or downloaded. The brown noise, the green noise, the rain, the soft rain, the ocean and the five synth themes are all produced by arithmetic while you listen.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no attribution services, no cloud services, no third-party SDKs that gather anything. Nobody receives data about you from this app, because the app does not have any and could not send it.

If you bought the app from Apple's App Store, Apple handles that transaction under its own privacy policy, and the developer never sees your payment details.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, deletion and portability apply to data a company holds about you. No data about you is held. The settings on your device are yours already: change them in the app, or delete the app to remove them completely.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here rather than quietly absorbed. The intention is that this page never needs to say anything more complicated than it does now.
